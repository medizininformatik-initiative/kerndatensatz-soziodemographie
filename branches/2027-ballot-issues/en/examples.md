## Examples

This Implementation Guide includes example instances for all profiles. The example titles and the descriptions stored in the example instances are in German (see [Translation Information](translationinfo.md)).

**Patient**

* [MII EXA SDD Patient](Patient-mii-exa-sdd-patient.md) - Example patient to whom the example data collections of this module refer

**Data Collection**

* [MII EXA SDD Datenerhebung](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-datenerhebung.md) - Grouping observation that references all individual sociodemographic observations of a patient

**Extended Origin**

* [MII EXA SDD Geburtsland Mutter](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-geburtsland-mutter.md) - Country of birth of the patient's mother
* [MII EXA SDD Geburtsland Vater](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-geburtsland-vater.md) - Country of birth of the patient's father

**Life Situation**

* [MII EXA SDD Betreuungssituation](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-betreuungssituation.md) - Current care/support situation
* [MII EXA SDD Haushaltsgroesse](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-haushaltsgroesse.md) - Number of persons permanently living in the person's household
* [MII EXA SDD Partnerschaft](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-partnerschaft.md) - Current partnership of the person
* [MII EXA SDD Schwerbehindertenausweis](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-schwerbehindertenausweis.md) - Presence of a severe disability certificate (Schwerbehindertenausweis) with degree of disability and markers (Merkzeichen)
* [MII EXA SDD Vertrauensperson](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-vertrauensperson.md) - Existence of at least one trusted contact person

**Socioeconomic Factors**

* [MII EXA SDD Soziooekonomische Faktoren](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-soziooekonomische-faktoren.md) - Generic observation of a socioeconomic factor
* [MII EXA SDD Ausbildung](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-ausbildung.md) - Highest vocational qualification
* [MII EXA SDD Berufliche Stellung](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-berufliche-stellung.md) - Occupational position in the main employment
* [MII EXA SDD Beschäftigungsstatus](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-beschaeftigungsstatus.md) - Current main activity
* [MII EXA SDD Einkommen](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-einkommen.md) - Average monthly net household income
* [MII EXA SDD Schulabschluss](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-schulabschluss.md) - Highest general school-leaving qualification
* [MII EXA SDD Schuljahre](Observation-mii-exa-sdd-schuljahre.md) - Total number of years of school attendance
