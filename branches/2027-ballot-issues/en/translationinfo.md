## Translation Information

This guide is written in **English** (the default language); **German** is the translation. English is therefore both the base rendering of the guide and the `/en/` rendering; use the language switcher at the top right to move between `/en/` and `/de/`.

Translated pages live under `input/translations/de/pagecontent/` (same file name as the English page).

> **What is translated:**
* The guidance and documentation pages and the navigation menu are available in both English and German.
* The FHIR artifacts — profiles, the logical model, value sets, code systems and examples — are **not** translated. Their titles, descriptions and element definitions were authored in **German** and appear in German in both language versions, including the Artifacts Summary and the individual artifact pages.
* The tables on the pages Datasets and Descriptions and Terminology reproduce the information model in its original German wording.
* Headings and labels generated by the IG Publisher (e.g. on the artifact pages) follow the selected language.
